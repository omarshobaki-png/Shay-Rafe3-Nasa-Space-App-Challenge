## Dates and times
- The hackathon runs 14–15 Nov 2026. **Teams may not begin working on the challenges before the hackathon** (FAQ).
- The team's Project page opens **Sat 14 Nov at 9:00** local time (the Local Event's time zone). Submission closes **Sun 15 Nov at 23:59** local time. The local judging times may differ, so ask the Local Lead.
- Announcements: Global Nominees on 3 Dec 2026, Global Finalists and Honorable Mentions on 17 Dec 2026, Global Winners on 26 Jan 2027.

## Team
- A team has 1–6 members, all registered for the **same Local Event** and not waitlisted. They must appear on the team's Members tab. No members can be added after submission closes.
- Each person can be on one team, and each team works on one challenge.
- Participants under 18 must be registered by a parent or guardian and accompanied by them. The demo and project may not show any image, video, voice or likeness of anyone under 18.

## What to submit (Project page fields)
1. Project name.
2. High-level summary: what we built, how it answers the challenge, why it matters.
3. **Demo link:** a **video of 30 seconds or less** OR **a slide deck of 7 slides or fewer**, including the title and intro slides. Going over either limit makes the project ineligible for Global Judging.
   - Host it on YouTube, Drive or GitHub with a public link that needs no login.
   - Write in English. A video may be spoken in another language only with English subtitles.
4. **Final project link:** public, no password or login. The game is ideally hosted as a website.
   - For a new website, the guide recommends requesting a FortiGuard Web Filter classification so judges' networks don't block it.
5. Detailed description: what it does, how it works, its benefits, our goals, and the tools and languages used.
6. **Use of Super Intelligence (SI, the US government's new name for AI).** Judges don't see this field, but they do see the AI marks inside the project. The rules:
   - "SI should not be used to (come up with your idea of how to solve the challenge)." **The idea must be the team's own.**
   - AI-generated images and videos need a **visible watermark**. AI audio, code and data must be acknowledged in descriptive text and metadata.
   - Generated content may not use or modify NASA branding: logos, flags or mission identifiers.
7. **NASA data:** say exactly which NASA data and resources were used. **At least one is required to be eligible for a Global Award.**
8. Space Agency Partner data and other references: list every outside resource, including code, text and images, even open-source ones.
   - No copyrighted material without permission.
   - 2026 partners include CSA, ESA, ISRO, JAXA, KASA, the UAE's MBRSC, UK Space Agency and the Turkish Space Agency, among others.
- Images up to 10 MB can go on the page itself. Videos and documents can't, so link them.
- To submit, tick the Terms box and the "all work is original, all external resources listed" box, then click **Submit for Judging**. Resubmitting is allowed until the deadline, and only the last save is judged.

## Demo script the guide suggests (30 s video)
- 0:00 who we are and the team name
- 0:02 the challenge and why we chose it
- 0:05 the project name and a tagline
- 0:10 how it works: show it, the user's experience, and how NASA open data made it possible
- 0:20–0:30 the future: what it could do for people

A slide deck must show the team name, members' names, the challenge, a picture of the project, the user experience, and how NASA data makes it possible.

## Judging: 5 criteria, each scored 1–5, at every level
- **Impact:** how big the problem is and how many people it reaches or inspires.
- **Creativity:** an original idea, or a meaningful improvement on an existing one.
- **Validity:** grounded in science, feasible, complete, usable and user-friendly.
- **Relevance:** fully answers this challenge. **NASA data must be core to the solution, not a superficial addition.**
- **Presentation:** the demo and the full project. The guide stresses this one: "pay equal attention to the final criterion."

## Global Awards (10)
- Best Use of Science
- Best Use of Data

- Galactic Impact
- **Best Mission Concept** ("the most plausible concept and design")
- Most Inspirational
- **Best Storytelling**
- Global Connection
- **Art & Technology**

The ones in bold fit a mission-design game best.

## Eligibility extras
- All team members must be confirmed at the same Local Event.
- Every required field must be filled in, in English.
- NASA's China funding restriction review applies.
